# Silly Wizards Guild — Canon

Frozen except by a dated decision in `aios/LOG.md`. Canon is supposed to freeze; staleness
here is not a defect and `aios check` never reports it.

## Identity

The Silly Wizards Guild is the umbrella organisation for the ventures Simon Attwater and
Filippos Arvanitakis build together. This repo is its website: a single hand-built page
with a hidden second layer beneath it.

**It is not a shopfront, not a pitch deck, and not a client-facing agency site.** The
second half matters more. The Guild is quiet infrastructure — a mark of authorship that
signs off venture sites and the end cards of films. Nobody is meant to arrive here to buy
something. They arrive because they saw the mark somewhere else and wondered.

This repo is the website. It is not the Guild, and it is not where the Guild's business
is recorded.

## Purpose

Someone who noticed the mark comes looking, and finds out what the Guild actually
believes — that as production gets cheap and abundant, human orchestration, taste and
narrative become the scarce thing. The site's job is to make that argument well, once,
and then get out of the way.

## Principles

1. **Quiet, not silent.** The Guild states a position. It does not sell, announce or
   chase. If a change makes the site louder, it is the wrong change.
2. **Two layers, and the second one is earned.** There is a surface and there is a way
   deeper. Discovery is the point, and it is deliberately not signposted.
3. **Content is abundant; meaning is scarce.** That is the argument the site exists to
   make, and every word on it either serves that or goes.
4. **Shared ventures carry the SW initials** — Silly Wizards, Standard Work, Seriously
   Wow, Strange Worlds, Sir Woof. Solo ventures break the pattern on purpose, because
   they are separately owned.
5. **Hand-built, single page, no framework.** It deploys on push and stays cheap to keep.

## Anti-patterns

- **Turning the Guild into a shopfront.** It has been raised and deliberately left
  undecided rather than drifted into. Adding services, prices or a contact funnel changes
  what the Guild is, and that is a decision for the founders, not a website update.
- **Signposting the hidden layer.** The site itself quotes a visitor saying "i didn't
  realise you could tap on the icons." That reaction *is* the product. A label explaining
  the icons would destroy the only interesting thing the page does.
- **A hand-written status file.** `_Project Notes.md`, on disk beside this repo, mixes
  genuine reference material with status claims that nothing keeps current. Status lives
  here now.

## What must not be touched

- **The hidden layer and the tappable icons.** Load-bearing because principle 2 is the
  only reason anyone stays longer than fifteen seconds.
- **The SW naming signature.** It is the quiet authorship mark across every shared
  venture, and it only works because it has never been explained.
- **Guild business material stays out of this repo.** Funding strategy, grant routes,
  equity positions, named client work and the Worldview deck live in the folders on disk
  and never in git — write the pointer, not the payload (AiOS canon §6). This binds
  whatever `Visibility:` says, and this repo is public.
